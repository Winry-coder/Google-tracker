# 🚀 Project Roadmap: Access Tracker Pulse

Access Tracker Pulse is a high-performance lead generation and Google Drive access management platform. This roadmap outlines the current achievements and future milestones.

---

## ✅ Phase 1: Core Infrastructure & Synchronization

- **Authentication**: Secure NextAuth implementation with admin-only dashboard access.
- **Database**: Robust Turso/Prisma schema for Users, Campaigns, Variants, and Sync Logs.
- **Sync Engine**: High-reliability Google Drive synchronization with rate-limit handling and reconcile logic.
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive tracking of all system and admin actions.

## ✅ Phase 2: Lead Generation & A/B Testing

- **Multi-Campaign Support**: Manage multiple folders and lead magnets from one dashboard.
- **A/B Testing Variants**: Create multiple versions (Variant A, Variant B) for each campaign to test headlines and CTAs.
- **Public Request Page**: Optimized landing pages with automatic traffic splitting and variant attribution.
- **Conversion Tracking**: Real-time tracking of views, leads, and conversion rates per variant.

## ✅ Phase 3: Dashboard & Management

- **Centralized Dashboard**: unified view of all leads across all campaigns.
- **Campaign Manager**: CRUD operations for campaigns and variants with a modern UI.
- **Bulk Operations**: Efficiently manage and cleanup leads in bulk.
- **CSV Export**: One-click lead export for CRM and marketing tool integration.

## 🚧 Phase 4: Automation & Expansion (Current Focus)

- [ ] **Automated Welcome Emails**: Instant delivery of resources via Postmark/SendGrid integration.
- [ ] **Webhook Integrations**: Sync leads to Zapier, HubSpot, or custom endpoints in real-time.
- [ ] **Advanced Analytics**: Time-series charts and growth trend visualizations.
- [ ] **User Enrichment**: Automatically fetch company and social information for new leads.
- [ ] **Team Collaboration**: Multiple admin accounts with role-based access control.
